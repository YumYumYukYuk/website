Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 30, 2026 

Company: YumYumYukYuk Ltd.

Introduction

Welcome to Clay Jam!

We’re YumYumYukYuk, and we care about your privacy. This policy explains, in simple language, what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

We designed Clay Jam so you can enjoy the game without giving us personal details. We only collect the minimum information needed to keep the game running smoothly and understand how players use it.

What Information We Collect

1. Anonymous analytics data

We use analytics tools (such as Unity Analytics, Firebase, or similar services) to understand things like:

  • How many people play the game
  • Which levels are popular
  • How often the game crashes
  • Basic device information (like model, OS version, country)

This data is anonymous. We cannot identify you as a person.

2. No personal accounts

We do not collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email
  • Your phone number
  • Your address
  • Any government ID
  • Photos or contacts
  • Payment information (we don’t process payments directly)

3. No location tracking

We do not track your precise location.

Analytics may tell us the country your device is in — but not your exact location.

4. No data from children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Why We Collect Data

We collect anonymous analytics so we can:

  • Fix bugs
  • Improve performance
  • Understand what players enjoy
  • Make better design decisions
  • Detect crashes or technical issues
  • Keep the game fair and safe

We do not sell your data.

We do not use your data for advertising profiles.

Who We Share Data With

We only share anonymous analytics with trusted service providers who help us run the game. These may include:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Cloud service providers

These companies are not allowed to use the data for anything other than helping us operate the game.

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Your Rights (GDPR & similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as:

  • The right to know what data we collect
  • The right to ask us to delete your data
  • The right to opt out of analytics (if your device or OS supports it)

Data Storage

We keep anonymous analytics data only as long as we need it to run and improve the game.

We do not store personal profiles or accounts.
