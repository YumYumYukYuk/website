1. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data in a lawful and fair way. The data we collect is limited to what is relevant and necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

1.1 Data You Provide to Us

Neither Clay Jam Classic nor Clay Jam requires a user account.

We may collect personal data that you choose to provide when you contact us for support, send feedback, respond to surveys, or otherwise communicate with us. This may include:

  • your email address;
  • your name or nickname, if you provide one;
  • the content of your message;
  • screenshots, attachments, or diagnostic details that you choose to send;
  • our communication records with you.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data, real-world identity documents, private contact details, or other information that is not needed for your request.

1.2 Data Collected in Both Games

When you use either Clay Jam Classic or Clay Jam, we and our service providers may automatically collect limited data needed to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the game, including:

  • Basic Device and Technical Data: device model, manufacturer, operating system, system language, time zone, screen information, CPU information, storage information, network information, app version, and similar technical information.
  • Gameplay and Usage Data: game access time, session information, level progress, scores, achievements, play time, feature usage, and other gameplay or usage information.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: crash logs, error reports, performance events, diagnostic events, and similar technical information.
  • Approximate Location Data: approximate country or region derived from technical information such as IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Analytics Identifiers: app instance IDs, randomly generated user IDs, or similar identifiers used for analytics, diagnostics, crash reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

1.3 Clay Jam Classic (ad-free)

For Clay Jam Classic:

  • we do not show ads;
  • we do not use advertising identifiers for advertising;
  • we do not collect ad interaction data;
  • we do not offer in-app purchases;
  • we do not collect purchase validation data.

Clay Jam Classic only collects limited analytics, diagnostics, crash, gameplay, and basic device data so we can operate, maintain, and improve the game.

1.4 Clay Jam

For Clay Jam, we may also collect:

  • Advertising Identifiers: advertising identifiers such as IDFA or GAID where available, permitted, and consented where required.
  • Ad Interaction Data: data about advertisements shown in Clay Jam, such as ad impressions, ad views, clicks, rewards, ad placement, ad performance, and related measurement or fraud-prevention information.
  • Ad Mediation Data: data needed to request, serve, measure, limit, and optimize ads through advertising networks and mediation partners.
  • Purchase Data: if you make an in-app purchase through Google Play or the App Store, we may receive purchase-related information from the relevant app marketplace, such as product purchased, transaction time, transaction status, order or receipt identifiers, storefront or country information, and encrypted purchase validation data. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

1.5 Data We Receive from Third Parties

For both games, we may receive information from analytics providers, crash reporting providers, app marketplaces, or other service providers where needed to operate the game, provide support, maintain security, or comply with law.

For Clay Jam, we may also receive information from advertising partners, mediation providers, measurement providers, privacy and consent management providers, and app marketplaces where needed to show ads, measure ad performance, manage consent, prevent fraud, verify purchases, or comply with law.

Data handled directly by Google Play, the App Store, or other third-party platforms is also governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

1.6 Sensitive Personal Data

Our games are not designed to collect sensitive personal data, such as precise location, health data, biometric data, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, or government identifiers. If we become aware that sensitive personal data has been provided to us by mistake, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

3. Advertising

Clay Jam Classic (ad-free)

Clay Jam Classic does not show ads. We do not use advertising identifiers, ad mediation, ad networks, or ad interaction data in Clay Jam Classic.

Clay Jam

Clay Jam is supported by advertising. We work with advertising networks, mediation providers, measurement providers, and related technology partners to show ads and measure their effectiveness.

Clay Jam may show:

  • personalized or targeted ads where permitted and consented where required;
  • non-personalized or contextual ads;
  • rewarded ads or other ad formats used to support the game.

For ad delivery, ad mediation, measurement, optimization, fraud prevention, and frequency capping, we may share:

  • Advertising Identifiers;
  • Basic Device and Technical Data;
  • Ad Interaction Data;
  • approximate location derived from IP address.

Advertising partners may process certain data as independent controllers, joint controllers, or service providers depending on the specific processing activity and applicable law. Their processing may be governed by their own privacy policies.

For users in the EEA and UK, personalized advertising is based on consent where required. We use a consent management platform, such as Google UMP, to request and manage advertising choices. You may withdraw or change consent at any time through the in-game privacy settings where available. If you do not consent to personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized or contextual ads.

For users in the United States, some disclosures of identifiers or ad interaction data to advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under certain privacy laws, even though we do not sell personal data for money. Where required, you may opt out using in-game privacy settings, a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" control where available, your device settings, or by contacting us.

4. In-App Purchases

Clay Jam Classic (ad-free)

Clay Jam Classic does not offer in-app purchases.

Clay Jam

Clay Jam may offer in-app purchases. If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play, the App Store, or another applicable app marketplace. We do not receive your payment card details.

We may receive purchase validation data from the app marketplace so we can:

  • verify the purchase;
  • deliver purchased content or features;
  • prevent purchase fraud;
  • provide purchase-related support;
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting, or platform requirements.

We may share personal data with:

Where international transfers require legal safeguards, we rely on appropriate mechanisms such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, Data Privacy Framework certifications, contractual protections, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

7. Data Security and Data Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Examples include:

  • support communications may be kept while needed to respond to your request and maintain records;
  • analytics, gameplay, crash, and diagnostic data may be retained according to our retention settings and the retention practices of our service providers;
  • for Clay Jam, advertising data may be retained according to our retention settings and the retention practices of our advertising partners;
  • for Clay Jam, purchase records may be kept as needed for purchase validation, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, and legal requirements;
  • data may be retained longer where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

When personal data is no longer needed, we will delete it, anonymize it, or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Clay Jam Classic (ad-free)

Clay Jam Classic is available for all ages, including children. It does not show ads, does not offer in-app purchases, does not use advertising identifiers for ads, and does not serve personalized or targeted advertising.

Clay Jam Classic collects limited analytics, diagnostics, crash, gameplay, and basic device data to support the internal operation of the game, including maintaining functionality, understanding performance, fixing bugs, and improving gameplay.

We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, precise location, photos, contacts, or other personal details from children in Clay Jam Classic unless a parent or guardian contacts us and chooses to provide that information.

Clay Jam

Clay Jam is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 through Clay Jam, and we do not knowingly serve personalized or targeted advertisements to children where prohibited by applicable law or platform policy.

Parent and Guardian Requests

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that we have collected personal data from a child in a way that is not described in this Privacy Policy, or served targeted advertising to a child in violation of this Privacy Policy or applicable law, please contact us. If we confirm that such data has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise comply with applicable law.

Residents of certain US states may have additional privacy rights, including the right to know, access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of personal data, opt out of targeted advertising, opt out of sale or sharing, and appeal certain decisions.

Clay Jam Classic does not sell or share personal data for advertising and does not use targeted advertising.

Clay Jam may disclose advertising identifiers or other personal data to advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising where permitted. You may opt out of such sale, sharing, or targeted advertising through in-game privacy settings where available, through device advertising settings, or by contacting us.

If we deny a request and applicable law gives you a right to appeal, you may appeal by contacting us using the details in Section 10.
