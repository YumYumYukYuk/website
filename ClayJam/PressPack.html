About The Studio

YumYumYukYuk are a new studio building gloriously odd, wildly creative games with a unique handmade feel. They aim to bring hope and kindness to the world through games that bring people together.

Their games are family friendly, but with layers of irony and cheerful subversiveness that span generations. At a time when cynicism and greed are fashionable, YumYumYukYuk are proud to stand up for togetherness and inclusivity.

YumYumYukYuk are platform agnostic. First and foremost, their focus is on creating beautifully bizarre worlds where players feel safe, inspired and creative. Whichever platform is the right one to achieve that is the right choice.

YumYumYukYuk. Deliciously ridiculous.

About the Team

YumYumYukYuk isn't just a new studio, it's a homecoming. The company was founded by three industry veterans who first joined forces over a decade ago to create Clay Jam, the critically acclaimed mobile classic loved for its tactile, squishy world and handmade charm.

Though their careers led them to leadership roles at powerhouses like Rovio, Aardman, and Unity, the pull of those early days - of building odd, heartfelt worlds from scratch - never faded.

After years of guiding major studios and shaping prominent games, they’ve reunited to do what they do best: get their hands dirty making the kind of gloriously strange games that first defined them. At YumYumYukYuk, they’ve finally come home to their passion.

About Clay Jam

Clay Jam is a family-friendly action game, entirely handmade using claymation. You control a pebble rolling down a hill, squashing clay monsters, growing big and trying to defeat the fearsome Bully Beasts.

The game has been downloaded more than 10 million times, has been nominated for a BAFTA and has thousands of 5 star user reviews.

It was originally made by the three founders of YumYumYukYuk. YumYumYukYuk now owns the rights to all Clay Jam IP and will re-release the original game on March 27th 2026 as a paid app on iOS and Android.

About the Fans

Clay Jam has been lying dormant for many years. While Chris, Iain and Michael always dreamed of getting the team back together, it never quite managed to happen.

However, in early 2026 they finally dipped back in to the world of Clay Jam and were amazed to find thousands of messages waiting for them from players.

Many were asking where the game had gone.

Many recalled memories from their childhoods of playing the game, about how it had inspired them to create clay models, or become artists, or just to be confident in living their dreams. Others talked about the times they'd spent connecting with sisters, brothers and parents through the game, or about how it was the go-to game for Christmas and Thanksgiving.

Many were parents who wrote about how it had helped their children through difficult times, with learning difficulties, inspired them to create art and stories or just the joy that it had brought them.

Some had created their own Clay Jam Discord channels, fan sites, petitions to bring the game back and made their own ports on to other hardware.

Many just loved the game.

For the team, it was incredibly inspiring and connected with their shared love for the game. Thanks to these messages, the choice was clear - they had to bring Clay Jam back; they had to make games with passion; they had to make games that made the world more connected, that made kindness cool again.

Bios & Quotes

Chris Roe
Chris is the creative force responsible for giving the studio's gloriously odd worlds their distinctive, handmade soul, as he did so successfully with Clay Jam.

A BAFTA-nominated art director with over 20 years of experience, Chris' unique style has taken him from the legendary halls of Lionhead Studios to the Oscar-winning sets of Aardman and the heady heights of Sky Kids. He has consistently managed to bring "strange" to the mainstream.

With YumYumYukYuk , I can build worlds that feel handmade, where every character has a little bit of soul in them. Weird, heartfelt ideas spark the imagination of people and bring them together. That's what we're about.

Iain Gilfeather
Iain is the architectural brain ensuring the studio's wildest creative ideas don't just look good - they actually work.

With over 25 years of experience, leading companies, navigating investment and on the frontier of emerging tech, Iain is a rare breed: a leader who is at home with corporate strategy and business operations yet still loves getting his hands dirty coding games.

YumYumYukYuk is me getting back to what I love. Clay Jam reached millions because it’s squishy and strange and it’s heartwarming to see players willing us on to bring it back.

Michael Movel
Michael is the creative strategist who knows how to take gloriously odd ideas and introduce them to millions of players.

With 30 years in the industry - including design director on Angry Birds 2 and creative director at Nordeus - his career is defined by a unique duality: a lifelong dreamer of "strange stories and undiscovered worlds" and a battle-tested leader with years spent at the pinnacle of mobile gaming.

With Clay Jam, we proved that strange games with a good heart appeal to millions of people. The industry has changed, but that hunger hasn't. We're here to feed it again - with everything we've learned along the way.
