Clay Jam is coming back!

We’ve been away for a while and we’ve let things slip a bit. We’re sooo sorry.

But not long ago, we started looking at all the places Clay Jam lived, and saw people still posting messages and talking about it. It was lovely. It brought a tear to our collective eye. Thank you. Really. It means so much.

So we’re bringing Clay Jam back to the stores again! We’re not sure exactly when or how yet, but it will happen. Feel free to let us know what you think!

We’ll be keeping everyone up to date here and:

Thanks again for sticking with us. This second chance exists because of all of you lovely people.

Let’s roll!

Chris, Iain and Michael
